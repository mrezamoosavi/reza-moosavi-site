About

I find where AI and data can move the business, then build teams that own the answer.

For more than 10 years I've designed and delivered AI and data solutions in regulated financial services across Canada and the US. I look for value on three sides at once: customers, through less friction and stronger protection; the organization, through lower cost and higher profit; and its people, through more efficient internal work, with analysis that used to take weeks now done in hours. Every solution is evidence-based and in line with the organization's long-term strategy.

The best solutions are built by teams who own them end to end. I set the direction and the architecture, then give each person full ownership of their stream, a sustainable pace, and growth matched to their own career goals. That's how I turned a BAU analytics team at TD into an in-house AI and data science capability.
